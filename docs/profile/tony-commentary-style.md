## Commentary principles

- Be specific to the post.
- Add a genuine observation, not generic praise.
- Prefer thoughtful, human comment starters over polished AI-sounding paragraphs.
- Ask a useful question when it opens a real conversation.
- Do not sound promotional.
- Do not pretend expertise Tony does not have.
- Do not copy-paste generated text without Tony editing it.
